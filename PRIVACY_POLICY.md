# Privacy Policy for SmartStock

**Effective Date:** August 17, 2026

SmartStock ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as a utility tool for microstock contributors, photographers, and videographers. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you may provide while using our application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect and Process

### A. Media Files and Metadata
- **Local Storage:** Media files (photos, videos) and generated metadata (titles, descriptions, keywords) are processed and stored locally on your device.
- **Photo Library Access:** The App requests access to your Photo Library solely to allow you to select and upload media files to stock agencies or export them.

### B. Third-Party Cloud and AI Integrations
- **Google Photos & Google Drive:** If you choose to connect Google Photos or Google Drive, the App accesses your media files via official Google APIs exclusively to display thumbnails and import chosen files into your upload queue. We do not store or share your Google account credentials.
- **AI Providers (Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude):** If you use AI analysis features, image thumbnails and prompt text are sent directly from your device to the respective AI provider's API using your own API key or demo session. No images or prompt logs are collected or retained by SmartStock servers.

### C. Stock Agency Credentials (FTP/FTPS/SFTP)
- Credentials and login details for stock agencies (e.g., Shutterstock, Adobe Stock, Getty Images) are securely stored in your device's Apple Keychain. They are transmitted directly to the respective stock agency servers via encrypted protocols and are never routed through or stored on third-party servers.

---

## 2. Data Security
We implement Apple Keychain and iOS sandboxing technologies to ensure that your API keys, login credentials, and local files remain private and secure on your device.

---

## 3. Children's Privacy
SmartStock does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.
